Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7 2026

SaveMateKSA uses TikTok authorization to publish content to its own authorized TikTok account.

The application may access the authorized TikTok account identifier username access token and content publishing permissions.

This information is used only to authenticate the account and publish content requested by the authorized account owner.

SaveMateKSA does not sell personal information. It does not share TikTok account data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Access tokens are stored securely and are used only for authorized publishing operations.

Users can revoke application access through their TikTok account settings. They may also contact SaveMateKSA through its official social media accounts to request deletion of stored authorization data.

This policy may be updated when the application or platform requirements change.
